## Independent calculations and proof checks
- For an equal chord pair AB=CD in a single circle O, OA=OB=OC=OD are radii; triangles OAB and OCD satisfy SSS, yielding equal central angles. Equal central angles imply the chord equality by SAS.
- For A(0,0), B(6,0), C(0,8), the right angle at A gives hypotenuse BC of length 10. Its midpoint M(3,4) has MA=MB=MC=5, thus circumradius=5 and circumcentre at M.
