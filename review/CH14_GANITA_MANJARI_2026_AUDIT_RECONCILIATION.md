## Editorial/math constraints checked

- Right cone surface area uses slant height \`l\`; volume uses perpendicular height \`h\`.
- For cone/hemisphere capacities, the correct surfaces and geometry depend on whether the base is open or covered.
- Archimedes' sphere-area equality with the enclosing cylinder is a theorem, **not proved** simply by evaluating \`2πr(2r)\`; original geometric motivation is included without pretending that substitution establishes the theorem.
- The 1:2:3 cone:sphere:cylinder ratio assumes the same radius and **cone/cylinder height 2r**.
- Cylinder with radius increased 10% and fixed volume requires height factor \`100/121\` (decrease \`2100/121%\`), while **uniform** 10% 3D scaling produces volume factor \`1.1³\`. Do not confuse the two.
- Cuboid integer solutions assume **positive integer edge lengths** and factor out permutations.
- Painted-cube counts assume each of the \`n³\` pieces has side 1 unit, with \`n≥2\`, original six faces painted **before** slicing.
- Surface area of cut pieces increases **when they are separated**, exposing the cut surfaces.
- For whole rods cast without material loss, keep only the integer floor of volume ratio.
- Store π exactly through intermediate steps unless the exercise specifies a decimal approximation.
