# Class 9 CBSE English, NCERT Kaveri 2026–27, Unit 1: audit reconciliation

## Sources and decision

Primary curriculum reference: [NCERT Kaveri Unit 1, iebe101.pdf](https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/iebe101.pdf), 32-page unit containing Sudha Murty's *How I Taught My Grandmother to Read* and Subramania Bharati's *Bharat Our Land*. A Gemini report supplied 14 October 2026 was used as a **candidate checklist**, not as authority. Correctness was independently compared with the official PDF text, especially printed pp. 9–16, 19–20, 23–30 and 32.

The original dedicated note module had **11 sections, 77 paragraphs and 54 worked examples**, plus the legacy eight-unit overview layer. This patch keeps the 11 original section titles and their previous example order, retaining existing subtopic keys. New examples are appended.

## Corrections and additions

* Corrects Part II true/false item 3 to **True** in both teaching text and a complete six-item worked answer; NCERT describes the child narrator as grandmother's first reading teacher, not the other way around.
* Adds complete six-row cause-and-effect reasoning, prose Extract 1 and Extract 2 subanswers, and guided answers to the seven prose reflection questions.
* Adds NCERT pre-reading word matches, ten correct prefixes (including **unimportant** and **discontinue**), sample prefixed-word sentences, five binomial sentences and six idiom examples.
* Adds complete simple-past/past-perfect sentence pairs A–E and financial-planning cloze A–J, with chronological explanations.
* Adds an original complete three-paragraph letter to a local newspaper editor with sender, date, five-word subject, recipient, salutation and formal closing. Preserves its paragraph separation in the focused reader.
* Adds turncoat speaking Topic 1 and concisely connects NLMA/DAE, NCFE, ELP and CCRT to the textbook's extension work.
* Clarifies that Bharati's recurring praise closes **stanzas 1 and 3**, not stanza 2; identifies personification of Bharat alongside Ganga; notes **no regular rhyme scheme in the printed English translation**, rather than assuming ABAB.
* Supplies the ten-term poem summary cloze, five extract answers, five critical reflection prompts, pre-reading vocabulary and anthem geography, suffix examples and an original travel paragraph satisfying all five prompts.

## Reconciliation judgment

The central comprehension and literary explanations were already sound. They were retained instead of rewritten. Exact textbook questions and source details were checked for genuine gaps. Several model sentences and prose/poetry interpretations are original illustrative answers, not official NCERT marking schemes. The model letter and travel paragraph are original.

## Deferred source limitations

The two listening tasks refer to teacher transcripts on full-textbook pp. 259–260, **outside** the 32-page unit PDF. No exact listening blanks or speaker mappings were invented. The full-textbook listening scripts and human teacher/editorial visual review remain outstanding. Do not mark the material officially certified. Class 9 English Units 2–8 still use their legacy overview notes.
