## 11 lessons in the full-note module
1. Prose orientation: writer/narrator, north Karnataka, Triveni, Karmaveera, Kashi Yatre.
2. Prose Part I: grandmother's dependency, missed episode, delayed access, social expectations, emotional turning point.
3. Prose Part II: age 62, Saraswati Puja deadline, rigorous tuition, foot-touching reversal, gift and independent reading.
4. Character studies and close-reading evidence; role reversal, education and dignity.
5. NCERT prose cause/effect, true/false, passage MCQs, critical reflection and model answers.
6. Vocabulary and grammar: binomials, negative prefixes, learning idioms, past simple versus past perfect.
7. Adult-literacy letter to editor, turncoat speech and digital-listening interpretation.
8. Subramania Bharati's poem: stanza-level movement from geography to art and philosophy.
9. Poetic devices: refrain, imagery, symbolism, personification, hyperbole, allusion, rhetorical questions, hyphenation and suffixes.
10. Poem critical reflection, symbol-pair models, poetry reading and descriptive place writing.
11. Integrated exam practice, original solutions and revision checklist.
