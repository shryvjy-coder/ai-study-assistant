# StudyAI — CBSE Class 9 English Kaveri, Unit 1 (2026–27): source and upgrade report

## Authoritative sources

- Official NCERT Grade 9 English *Kaveri*, Chapter 1 / Unit 1, PDF **`iebe101.pdf`**: https://ncert.nic.in/textbook/pdf/iebe101.pdf. It covers Sudha Murty, *How I Taught My Grandmother to Read*, and Subramania Bharati, *Bharat Our Land*. Official PDF was read, including the story, poem and prose/poetry exercises. Textbook PDF contains 32 PDF pages; book numbering begins with introductory pages and numbered teaching pages.
- Official CBSE academic 2026–27 Class IX English R1/R2 syllabus: https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/CurriculumMain27/SecPart1/English_LL_SecP1IX_2026-27.pdf. R1: **20 marks reading, 30 writing+grammar, 30 literature, 20 internal assessment**. R2 differs in specific passage/task types despite sharing the textbook.
- CBSE notification dated 9 April 2026 confirms that the old Class IX English Communicative course has been discontinued, replaced by the new English course using *Kaveri*: https://cbseacademic.nic.in/web_material/Notifications/2026/40_Notification_2026.pdf .
- StudyAI's existing `cbse-ncert-deep-notes-09.js` already has **eight English unit identifiers** `iebe101.pdf` … `iebe108.pdf`. These IDs and the seven later units' notes remain untouched.

## Source alignment: prose

- Rural north Karnataka; a twelve-year-old narrator and her grandmother **Krishtakka (Avva)**.
- Triveni's serial novel **Kashi Yatre**, appearing in Kannada magazine **Karmaveera**, and the elder heroine's philanthropic choice.
- Grandmother's emotional crisis when the narrator spends a full week away at a wedding and cannot read the magazine; social constraints on earlier schooling; distinction between economic comfort and reading independence.
- Learning Kannada at **62**, setting **Saraswati Puja during Dassara** as a deadline, diligent practice and intergenerational teacher/student reversal.
- Dassara outcome: a gifted `Kashi Yatre` book; the grandmother's demonstration of reading; gifts, touching the young teacher's feet and mutual respect.
- NCERT prose vocabulary and skills: binomials, prefixes, learning-related idioms, chronology with simple past/past perfect, listening about digital literacy, turncoat speaking exercise, letter to editor on adult-literacy volunteering.

## Source alignment: poetry

- *Bharat Our Land*, an ode by **Subramania Bharati**.
- Himavant/Himalayas, Ganga, Upanishads; warriors, sages, music, Buddha and Brahma-knowledge.
- Refrain, rhetorical questions, personification, hyperbole, symbolism, allusion, imagery, hyphen/dash pauses, mood and tone.
- Do not assign a confident regular rhyme pattern without a verified sound analysis; rhyme isn't required for ode structure.
- NCERT-inspired, newly written original poem and story model responses. **Neither the prose passages nor poem stanzas are republished in StudyAI**.

## New authored content and interface

- **17 topical lessons** combining 141 independently written paragraphs. Each lesson includes 2 specific titled subtopics for **34 explanatory subtopics**.
- **79 solved examples**, including original critical responses and a complete 135-word student model letter to the editor.
- **74 original self-check questions** with native accessible `<details>` answer toggles.
- Total available focused learning pages: 17 main explanatory pages + 34 named subtopics + 79 individually authored worked-answer pages = **130** selectable and checkable pages, all linked to real writing.
- Shared existing `StudyAIClass9Outline`, `StudyAILessonReader`, `StudyAIProgress` expanded from Maths/Science to authored English units. Progress remains keyed to original `STUDY_DATA` entry IDs under `state.studyaiLessonProgress` and persists across reload. This doesn't recreate the curriculum or mutate earlier completed records.
- English Units 2–8 **remain legacy overviews** until upgraded. They are not falsely labelled fully audited, and their lesson sidebar explicitly displays an upgrade-in-progress explanation rather than stale Unit 1 checkboxes.
- Mobile responsive and keyboard-accessible source links and practice disclosures.

## Scope and residual limitations

- The project is progressing **unit by unit**, beginning with Unit 1; **do not claim all eight English units have exam-sufficient notes yet**.
- R1/R2 vary; users follow their school's route. Long-form prose and poetry answers are newly authored, not official marking schemes. Certain interpretation questions permit more than one defensible answer.
- The exact answers to the *listening* exercise are intentionally not fabricated without a recording/verified transcript.
- Thorough external teacher and visual editorial certification remains **outstanding**.
- CI gate must verify new Unit 1 browser regression AND existing Maths, Science, Cambridge, PostgreSQL, syntax jobs before merge and Render deployment.
